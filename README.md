# Netflix-Content-Analysis

## 📌Project Overview
This project analyzes Netflix Movies and TV Shows to understand the content available on Netflix.

The project includes data cleaning, SQL analysis, and Power BI visualization.

## 🎯Objective
The main objective is to analyze Netflix content and find useful insights about:
- Movies and TV Shows
- Release years
- Countries
- Genres
- Ratings
- Directors

## 🧹Data Cleaning
Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.

## 🗄️SQL Analysis
The cleaned data was imported into MySQL.

SQL queries were used to analyze:
- Total content
- Movies vs TV Shows
- Content by release year
- Top 10 countries
- Top 10 genres
- Top 5 ratings
- Top 10 directors
  
## 📊Power BI
Power BI was used for data visualization and reporting.

The report includes:
- KPI cards
- Movies vs TV Shows donut chart
- Content by release year
- Top 10 countries
- Top 10 genres
- Top 5 ratings
- Top 10 directors
- Interactive slicers

## 💡Key Insights
- Total Content: 8,784
- Movies: 6,112 (69.58%)
- TV Shows: 2,672 (30.42%)
- United States has the highest content contribution
- India is the second-highest country contribution
- TV-MA is the most common rating
